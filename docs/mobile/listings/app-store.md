## Description

Milōn is the finance workspace for a business and the accountant who looks after it.

Connect QuickBooks or Xero. Sage is coming soon. Milōn Financial Manager, Milōn Analyst and Milōn Advisor read the books and draft the work. You review it and sign off.

The app opens that workspace on iPhone. You get the health view, the 13-week cash view, and the next moves, for a person to correct.

What Milōn does not do

Milōn reads the accounting books. It does not connect to the bank.

It does not prepare a tax return, and it does not file one.

It does not promise a filing date.

Figures are as current as the last sync or upload from the accounting file.

The books stay in QuickBooks or Xero.

A plan for a firm is arranged on the website. This listing does not sell a plan.

## Review notes

The app is an iPhone client for an account that already exists. Sign in with the demo account named in the review notes. That account holds seeded sample books. It is not a real client.

Plans are arranged on the website, outside the app. The demo account is already on a plan, so review can open the workspace.

Account deletion is inside the app: Settings, then type DELETE. Use a disposable login for that test if the seeded demo account should survive review.

Export compliance: the app uses HTTPS only. ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false.

Age rating: no unrestricted web access as a browser, no gambling, no user-generated public feed. Confirm the live questionnaire.
