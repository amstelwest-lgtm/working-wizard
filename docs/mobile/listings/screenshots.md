# Screenshot plan

Capture these from the demo account only. The demo account holds seeded sample books. Never a real client, a real firm ledger, or a real receipt.

The demo login is created by a human in the production project and stored in App Store Connect review notes and the Play testing instructions. It does not go in git.

## Device sizes

Phone only. The shell targets iPhone and a normal Android phone.

- Play: at least four phone screenshots, portrait 9:16. Each side between 320 and 3840 px. A 1080×1920 capture is enough.
- App Store: the iPhone size App Store Connect requires on the day of upload. At the time of this draft that includes the 6.9-inch display at 1320×2868. Confirm in the form. Do not invent a second size.
- No 7-inch, 10-inch, or iPad set.

Play feature graphic, 1024×500: white field, the MILŌN wordmark. Gold only inside that wordmark. No screenshot of a client.

## What every frame shows

- White and cobalt chrome. Navy text. Gold only in the MILŌN wordmark.
- The vertical navigation of the product. Do not crop in a bottom bar of faces.
- Avatars stay silent. No speech balloon, no caption painted onto an avatar.
- The sample business name the demo account was given, so a reviewer can see it is a sample.

## Frames, in order

1. The practice home for the demo firm, vertical nav visible, no client list from a real practice.
2. Health for the one seeded business on that demo account.
3. The 13-week cash view for that same seeded business.
4. Source documents for that business, with a sample file the human placed there. Not a photograph of a real invoice.
5. Talk with Milōn Financial Manager at `/talk/financial-manager`. That page is a labelled sample and connects no books. It still must not be a thread from a real person.

Do not shoot billing, a price, a card form, Settings deletion, or a live QuickBooks or Xero company.

## Who captures them

A human, signed in as the demo account, on a phone or a simulator of the phone size above. This repository does not contain the shots.
