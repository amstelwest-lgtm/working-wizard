## Title

Milōn: AI finance team

## Short description

Your AI finance team. Health, 13-week cash, and the next moves.

## Long description

Your AI finance team. You sign off.

Milōn is the finance workspace for a business and the accountant who looks after it. Connect QuickBooks or Xero. The team reads the books and drafts the work. You review it and sign off.

Milōn Financial Manager runs the team. It checks data quality from QuickBooks or Xero, keeps the 13-week cash view and the budget, tracks action points, and prepares accountant hand-offs.

Milōn Analyst reads the books. It drafts the health score, the ratios, and the variances.

Milōn Advisor drafts the next moves and the advisory deliverables, for you to correct and sign off.

What this is not

Milōn reads the accounting books. It does not connect to the bank.

Only the 13-week cash forecast looks forward. The rest is the books you have already kept, including the budget you are measuring against.

It does not prepare a tax return, and it does not promise a filing date.

Figures are as current as the last sync or upload from the accounting file.

The books stay in QuickBooks or Xero. Milōn is the workspace on top of them.
