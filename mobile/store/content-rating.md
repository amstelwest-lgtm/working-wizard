# Content rating draft (IARC / Play questionnaire)

Category in the store listing: Finance. The product is a finance and productivity workspace. These are the answers to give the questionnaire. Confirm the wording against the live form, which Google revises.

## Violence

None. No cartoon, fantasy, realistic, or sexual violence. No weapons.

## Sexuality

None. No sexual content, nudity, or dating.

## Language

None. No profanity and no crude humour as a feature.

## Controlled substances

None. No references to alcohol, tobacco, or drugs as content.

## Gambling

None. No simulated gambling, no cash-out, no prize draws.

## User-generated content and sharing

No public user-generated content and no public social feed.

People in one workspace can see the books, notes, and sign-offs for that workspace. An accountant sees a client only when that client is linked to their firm. There is no browse-all view and no sharing to a public audience. Do not tick unrestricted UGC, public profiles, or a social network.

No location sharing. The app does not read the device location.

## Digital purchases

The shell does not use Google Play Billing. A subscription, when it is offered, is paid on the website through Stripe Checkout, which opens in a Custom Tab. There are no loot boxes and no random items.

If the questionnaire asks whether users can purchase digital goods at all, answer yes and describe a software subscription sold on the website. If it asks only about Play in-app products, answer no. Read the sentence on the form. Do not claim the app is free of payment if Checkout is reachable.

## Miscellaneous

- Online: yes. The app loads https://www.milonfinance.com.
- Users interact with invited teammates inside an account: yes, privately, as above.
- Shares the user's personal information with them in the product: the account shows the signed-in person's own workspace. It is not a public directory.
- Horror, health-condition content, or mature themes: no.

Expected rating band: Everyone / PEGI 3 / IARC 3+, subject to the digital-purchase answer. Do not file until the live form matches this draft.
